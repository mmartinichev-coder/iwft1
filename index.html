IWouldFundThat, LLC

Retainer financing
for growth-company raises.

Growth companies raising $1M–$10M usually have to pay an investment bank retainer and fund investor outreach before any capital comes in. IWouldFundThat finances those two costs. Each company receives $200K: $100K retains an institutional investment bank to run the raise, and $100K pays for investor dinner seminars. The loan is repaid from the raise proceeds, and the lender receives an equity warrant in the company.
